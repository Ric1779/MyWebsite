About🧗🏽

April 9, 2024 · 2 min · Richards Britto

I’m Richards, a Deep Learning engineer driven by a deep curiosity about how we perceive, reason about, and interact with the world. My interests naturally pulled me toward maths, physics, computer graphics, and DL, because they all attempt to answer the same fundamental question: how does reality work? I’ve always been fascinated not just by solving problems, but by understanding things from first principles, whether exploring how humans developed mathematics, how logic emerges from the mind, or how machines interpret visual information.

I hold a master’s degree in Image Analysis and ML from Uppsala University and currently work as an AI & 3D Software Developer at IKEA, where my interests in DL, computer vision, and CGI naturally come together. But my curiosity for visual worlds started long before tech. Growing up, I travelled across India, and spending much of my time sketching, watching films and anime, and imagining stories, characters, and worlds of my own. That creative instinct still shapes how I think today, and it’s a big part of why I’m drawn to visual computing.

Outside of work, I enjoy football, powerlifting, and calisthenics. I see physical training as a way to build both discipline and awareness. I also read and reflect on philosophy and psychology, especially ideas around meaning and how humans try to understand reality. Over time, I’ve come to see purpose less as something given and more as something we actively shape through curiosity, creativity, relationships, and the ongoing pursuit of understanding.

At my core, I’m someone who simply wants to keep learning, about the world, about people, and about the strange experience of being alive within all of this.
